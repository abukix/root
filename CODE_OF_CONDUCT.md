# Code of Conduct

`/root` adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

The full text is available at the link above. This file records how it applies to `/root` and how to report concerns.

## Our commitment

Contributors, maintainers, and adopters of `/root` agree to:

- **Be kind and civil.** Disagree on technical substance, never on personalities.
- **Assume good faith.** Interpret ambiguous behavior charitably before escalating.
- **Focus on the work.** Reviews cite specific examples, not generalizations about the person.
- **Own mistakes.** When corrections land, thank the reviewer and move on.
- **Respect scope.** `/root` is one operator's authored curriculum; scope-expansion arguments belong in Discussions, not in interpersonal disputes.

## Scope

This code applies wherever the `/root` project is represented:

- The `abukix/root` GitHub repository (Issues, PRs, Discussions, Releases)
- Any future Slack workspace, mailing list, or event associated with `/root`
- Public conversations where you identify as a `/root` contributor or adopter

It does not apply to your unrelated private life or to disagreements in other projects, even projects the same maintainer runs.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation:

- **Not urgent, not private**: open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/abukix/root/discussions) tagged `conduct`.
- **Private or urgent**: email `me@abukix.dev`. All reports are read by the maintainer only.
- **Security-relevant** (a report that would expose someone if made public): see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) for the private-advisory path.

All reports are handled confidentially. The reporter's identity is not disclosed to the accused party without the reporter's consent.

## Enforcement

The maintainer investigates every report received. Response commitments:

- **Acknowledgment**: within 72 hours of receipt.
- **Investigation**: within 2 weeks for non-urgent, 72 hours for urgent.
- **Action**: proportional to the violation, ranging from a private note, to a formal warning, to temporary interaction restrictions, to a permanent ban from the project's spaces.

Per Contributor Covenant 2.1's enforcement ladder:

1. **Correction**: private written notice, clarifying what was wrong and what's expected.
2. **Warning**: a formal warning with consequences noted.
3. **Temporary ban**: a defined period of no interaction, private or public.
4. **Permanent ban**: no further participation in `/root`'s spaces.

The maintainer's decision is final for v0.x. Post-v1.0, an appeals process may be added if the maintainer set expands.

## Retaliation

Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation and is treated at the higher end of the enforcement ladder.
